# Office Operations and Multi-Artifact Work

## Meetings and actions

Distinguish agenda, notes, confirmed decisions, proposals, open questions and actions.
For each action retain an owner, deliverable, due date, status and dependency where known.
Do not assign an absent owner/date as though it were agreed. Keep names and terminology
consistent and remove transcript noise without erasing dissent or decision context.

## Project plans and SOPs

Define outcome, scope, stakeholders, work breakdown, dependencies, milestones, risks and
acceptance. Make tasks observable and sized for an owner. Show critical dependencies and
assumptions; a calendar without dependencies is not a reliable schedule. For SOPs define
prerequisites, roles, ordered steps, decision points, exceptions and records. Check that
another worker can follow the process without hidden context.

## Proposals and recurring reporting

Use reusable sections and named inputs while preserving the approved template. Separate
current period from historical comparison and forecast. Include reporting period and
version, source scope, highlights, risks, next actions and supporting detail. Reconcile
the same metric across all artifacts; do not hand-copy inconsistent values into slides.

## Delivery

Inventory requested outputs and their relationship: source workbook, summary report,
presentation and optional PDF. Use predictable filenames, native editable formats and
clear links. Preserve originals by default. Check required sections, owners/dates,
internal references, values, visual consistency and final Office validation status.
Return completed files and practical limitations. Do not send mail, calendar invites or
external messages unless the user authorized that action.
